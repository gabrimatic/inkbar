Inkbar

Select text in any Mac app and get a bar of actions for it: copy, search, translate, open a link, do a sum, look up a word, add it to your calendar, and more.

Download View on GitHub

What it does

A bar appears just above your selection and never takes focus from the app you are typing in. Apple Translation and Apple Intelligence do the language work on this Mac.

Every app

Works on selected text across your Mac, with per-app rules and a key-held mode.

Cards

Translate, Calculate, Count and Apple Intelligence results open a card with Copy and Replace.

Read ahead

A meeting gets Add to Calendar, someone's details get Add Contact, an error shows the likely fix.

Cleaning

Copy a link without its tracking parameters, and paste text without its odd spaces.

Your bar

Drag actions to reorder them, switch each one on or off, use number keys.

Private by default

Password fields and secure input are always skipped.

Install

Free and open source, for macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrimatic/inkbar/main/install.sh | bash

It checks the download's SHA-256 before it installs anything. Or download the disk image and see First Open: release builds are signed ad hoc, not notarized.

Privacy

Inkbar makes no network requests and never logs or stores what you select. Translation and Apple Intelligence run on this Mac. It needs Accessibility, and Reminders only when you add a task.
